Interesting Facts About New Zealand

New Zealand Wildlife

New Zealand is home to many unique animals. The kiwi is a flightless bird and an important national symbol. Kiwi are mostly nocturnal, which means they are active at night. Protecting native animals is essential for New Zealand's enviroment

New Zealand's Natural Environment

New Zealand has mountains, beaches, forests and volcanoes. Water is written scientifically as H2O, and clean water is important for people and wilflife. A square kilometre can be written as km2. Visitors should follow the rule: Take only photographs and leave only footprints

Caring for the Enviroment

People can help the environment by reducing waste, recycling and using reusable items. Instead of saying someone else will fix the problem, we should say everyone can make a difference. Even small actions, such as picking up litter, can help protect Aotearoa New Zealand.
